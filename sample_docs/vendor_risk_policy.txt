THIRD-PARTY VENDOR RISK MANAGEMENT POLICY

Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Policy Owner: Enterprise Risk Management
Version: 1.0

1. PURPOSE

This policy establishes requirements for identifying, assessing, approving, monitoring, and terminating third-party vendor relationships.

The objective is to reduce cybersecurity, operational, financial, compliance, privacy, and reputational risks associated with third parties.

2. SCOPE

This policy applies to vendors, contractors, consultants, service providers, software providers, cloud providers, and other third parties that provide products or services to the company.

3. VENDOR RISK CLASSIFICATION

Vendors must be classified according to their level of risk.

Low-risk vendors have limited access to company information and do not support critical business processes.

Medium-risk vendors may process internal company information or support important business operations.

High-risk vendors include vendors that:

- Process confidential or sensitive information.
- Access production systems.
- Store customer information.
- Support critical business processes.
- Provide significant technology infrastructure.
- Could materially disrupt company operations if unavailable.

4. DUE DILIGENCE

Risk assessments must be completed before entering into relationships with high-risk vendors.

Assessments may include:

- Information security questionnaires.
- Financial stability reviews.
- SOC 2 or similar assurance reports.
- Data privacy assessments.
- Business continuity documentation.
- Regulatory and compliance reviews.
- Cybersecurity controls assessments.

Medium-risk vendors should undergo a risk assessment based on the nature of the service.

5. CONTRACT REQUIREMENTS

Contracts involving sensitive company or customer information must include appropriate security and confidentiality requirements.

Where applicable, contracts should address:

- Data protection.
- Security incident notification.
- Audit rights.
- Business continuity.
- Regulatory requirements.
- Data destruction following contract termination.

6. ONGOING MONITORING

High-risk vendors must be reassessed at least annually.

Medium-risk vendors should be reassessed at least once every two years.

Vendor owners are responsible for reporting significant changes in vendor services or risk exposure.

7. SECURITY INCIDENTS

Vendors must notify the company of confirmed security incidents involving company information as soon as reasonably possible.

Critical vendor incidents must be escalated to Information Security, Legal, and Enterprise Risk Management.

8. VENDOR ACCESS

Vendor access to company systems must follow the company's access-control and privileged-access requirements.

Access must be limited to systems necessary for the vendor's contracted responsibilities.

Vendor access must be removed when the relationship ends.

9. TERMINATION

The business owner must notify Information Technology and Information Security when a vendor relationship ends.

Company information must be returned or securely destroyed according to contractual and regulatory requirements.

System credentials and remote access associated with the vendor must be disabled.

10. EXCEPTIONS

Exceptions to this policy must be documented and approved by Enterprise Risk Management and the appropriate business owner.

11. POLICY REVIEW

This policy must be reviewed at least annually and updated when significant changes occur to regulatory requirements, company operations, or the third-party risk environment.
