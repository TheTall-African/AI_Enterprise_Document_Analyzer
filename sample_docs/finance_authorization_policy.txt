FINANCE AUTHORIZATION POLICY

Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Policy Owner: Finance Department
Version: 1.0

1. PURPOSE

This policy establishes financial authorization requirements for company expenditures, purchases, reimbursements, contracts, and other financial commitments. The purpose is to ensure appropriate oversight, accountability, and separation of duties.

2. SCOPE

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, managers, and executives who initiate, approve, process, or review financial transactions on behalf of the company.

3. AUTHORIZATION LIMITS

Employees may approve routine business expenses up to $1,000 when the expense is within an approved departmental budget.

Managers may approve expenditures up to $10,000.

Department Directors may approve expenditures up to $50,000.

Any expenditure exceeding $50,000 requires approval from both the Chief Financial Officer (CFO) and the relevant Department Director.

Transactions exceeding $250,000 require approval from the CFO and Chief Executive Officer (CEO).

4. PURCHASE ORDERS

Purchases exceeding $5,000 must have an approved purchase order before goods or services are ordered.

Employees may not divide a purchase into multiple smaller transactions for the purpose of avoiding approval thresholds.

5. EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENTS

Employees must submit reimbursement requests within 30 days of the expense.

Receipts are required for individual expenses greater than $25.

Managers are responsible for reviewing reimbursement requests for accuracy and legitimate business purpose.

Employees may not approve their own reimbursement requests.

6. SEGREGATION OF DUTIES

Whenever practical, the individual requesting a financial transaction should not be the same individual approving and processing the transaction.

Employees responsible for issuing payments should not approve the underlying purchase.

7. EMERGENCY EXPENDITURES

Emergency expenditures may be processed without normal pre-approval when delaying the transaction could cause significant operational disruption or financial loss.

Emergency expenditures must be documented and submitted to the Finance Department for retrospective approval within five business days.

8. RECORD RETENTION

Documentation supporting financial transactions must be retained for a minimum of seven years.

Records must include invoices, approvals, purchase orders, receipts, and supporting documentation where applicable.

9. POLICY VIOLATIONS

Violations may result in suspension of financial authorization privileges and may be escalated to management, Internal Audit, Human Resources, or Legal depending on the severity of the violation.
