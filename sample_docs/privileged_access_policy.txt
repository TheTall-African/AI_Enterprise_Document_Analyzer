PRIVILEGED ACCESS MANAGEMENT POLICY
Effective Date: July 1, 2026

Policy Owner: Identity & Access Governance (IAG)

Version: 2.0

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes strict mandatory requirements for requesting, approving, vaulting, monitoring, auditing, and revoking privileged access across all enterprise information systems.

Privileged accounts grant administrative, root, or elevated permissions and represent critical security exposure. The objective is to enforce Zero Trust principles, mandate Just-In-Time (JIT) access where applicable, and mitigate insider and external cyber threats.

2. SCOPE
This policy applies globally to all employees, temporary workers, external contractors, third-party vendor representatives, system engineers, database administrators, and automated service accounts requiring elevated privileges on company infrastructure, cloud services, databases, network devices, and code repositories.

3. ACCESS APPROVAL & TIERED AUTHORIZATION
Privileged access is granted strictly based on Least Privilege and Need-to-Know principles. Approval workflows depend on system classification:

Standard Business / Non-Production Systems: Requires approval from the direct Line Manager and the System Owner.

Production & High-Risk Systems (PII/Financial/Core Infrastructure): Requires dual approval from the System Owner and the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) or designated IAG Delegate.

Permanent / Standing Privileged Access: Explicitly prohibited for human users. All privileged access must be temporary and auto-expiring.

4. ARCHITECTURE & VAULTING REQUIREMENTS
Privileged Access Management (PAM) Vault: All administrative credentials (passwords, SSH keys, API tokens) must be stored, rotated, and checked out via the enterprise PAM vault. Direct login without vault mediation is strictly prohibited.

Separation of Accounts: Users must maintain a standard user account for daily activities (email, browsing) and a distinct privileged account (priv_ prefix) for administrative duties.

Shared & Root Accounts: Built-in root/administrator accounts must be vaulted with automated password rotation after every use or every 24 hours. Shared logins are banned unless technically impossible, in which case session recording is mandatory.

5. AUTHENTICATION & SESSION CONTROL
Hardware-Based MFA: Phishing-resistant Multi-Factor Authentication (FIDO2/WebAuthn hardware keys) is mandatory for all privileged logins, including local and remote access.

Session Timeout: Privileged sessions automatically terminate after 15 minutes of inactivity.

Session Recording: Real-time video and command-level session recording is mandatory for all access to production environments, payment gateways, and core databases.

6. AUDITING, LOGGING & RETENTION
Log Scope: System logs must capture user ID, vaulted session ID, source IP address, exact commands executed, target system, and precise UTC timestamps.

Log Retention: Audit logs for all privileged activities must be streamed to a write-once Read-Many (WORM) central SIEM and retained for at least 24 months (7 years for financial/tax systems).

Automated Threat Monitoring: Real-time behavioral analytics must alert the SOC immediately upon detection of out-of-hours access, concurrent multi-location logins, or unauthorized privilege escalation commands.

7. ACCESS REVIEWS & RECERTIFICATION
Tier 1 (Production / Critical Systems): Access must be reviewed and recertified monthly by System Owners.

Tier 2 (General Enterprise Systems): Access must be reviewed quarterly.

Orphaned Access Remediation: Uncertified access rights automatically revoke within 48 hours following a passed review deadline. Evidence of review sign-off must be retained by IAG for regulatory auditing.

8. WORKFORCE CHANGES & TERMINATION SLAs
Involuntary Termination: Privileged credentials, active sessions, and access rights must be revoked immediately (within 1 hour) of notification from HR.

Voluntary Resignation: Access must be revoked at the official end of the final working day.

Role Change / Transfer: Privileged access tied to a previous position must be revoked within 24 hours (1 business day) of the effective transfer date.

9. THIRD-PARTY & VENDOR ELEVATED ACCESS
Vendor privileged access must be authorized by a Senior Director and restricted to dedicated Jump Hosts with mandatory session recording.

Vendor access window cannot exceed 8 consecutive hours per session request; permanent external access is strictly prohibited.

All vendor activities must be actively monitored in real time or reviewed post-session by a designated internal System Owner.

10. EMERGENCY ACCESS ("BREAK-GLASS")
Emergency administrative credentials are sealed in the PAM vault and restricted to active outages or P1 incidents.

Unlocking a break-glass account requires dual-custody authorization (two designated security leaders).

Post-Incident Audit SLA: Full audit logging and justification of break-glass usage must be formally submitted to the CISO and IAG within 24 hours of incident closure.

11. POLICY EXCEPTIONS & ENFORCEMENT
Exceptions: Temporary policy exceptions require a documented risk assessment, compensating security controls, and formal sign-off from the CISO and Enterprise Risk Committee. Exceptions expire after a maximum of 90 days.

Violations: Bypassing PAM controls, sharing credentials, or turning off session logging constitutes a critical security breach resulting in immediate access revocation, potential employment termination, and escalation to Legal and Regulatory Compliance.
